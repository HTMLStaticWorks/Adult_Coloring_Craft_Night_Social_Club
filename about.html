Why We Started

A different kind of night out.

Not every evening needs loud music, a screen, or a packed schedule. Color & Company was created for people who want to slow down, make something with their hands, and enjoy conversation without pressure.

Our guided creative nights bring together coloring, crafts, seasonal projects, and friendly company. Come with friends or arrive on your own — there is room at the table either way.
