Our Story

We Believe in Making Things.

Adults deserve a space to be creative, social, and offline. Welcome to Color & Company.

Why We Started.

Going out used to mean loud bars, expensive dinners, or sitting in a dark theater. We wanted something different — an evening where you could talk, relax, and actually make something with your hands.

Color & Company was built on a simple idea: creativity shouldn't stop when you grow up, and socialising shouldn't always involve a screen or a shout.

Philosophy

It's about the process, not perfection.

You don't need to be an artist to join. You just need a desire to make something and disconnect from the noise.

A Space Designed for Focus.

Every detail of Color & Company has been considered to help you drop into a flow state. Ambient lighting, comfortable seating, and premium tools provided at every table.

Our Core Values

Inclusivity

Everyone starts somewhere. We welcome absolute beginners and seasoned creators alike.

Analog Connection

A strict no-laptops policy during sessions ensures everyone is present in the room.

Quality Materials

We source only the best paper, pencils, and craft materials so your work feels premium.
