Arrive & Settle In

Walk in, find your name at the table, and settle into your creative space for the evening. Everything you need is waiting for you.

Choose Your Project

Browse the evening's selection and pick the project that speaks to you. No rules — just your instincts and the materials in front of you.

Create Freely

Follow guidance or go your own way. Premium pencils, markers, watercolors and craft tools are yours for the evening. Take your time.

Chat & Connect

Something about creating side-by-side makes conversation easy. Share the table, share the experience — make an unexpected connection.

Take It Home

Your creation belongs to you. Leave the studio with something made by your hands — proof that the evening was well spent.

Membership

A creative club for your calendar.

Membership at Color & Company gives you regular access to sessions, early booking, member-only events and a community of creative adults.

Monthly Session Access

Use your monthly session credits to book any regular sessions on the schedule. Credits roll over for one month.

Early Booking

Members get access to session booking before it opens to the general public — so you never miss a themed night.

Member-Only Nights

Exclusive sessions designed just for members. Seasonal previews, guest-led workshops, and special themed evenings.

Creation Gallery

Access your personal creation archive and browse the community gallery from your member dashboard.

Community Access

Join a community of creative adults who show up regularly, make things together, and keep coming back.

Guest Benefits

Bring a friend, partner, or colleague. Higher-tier plans include guest session passes each month.
